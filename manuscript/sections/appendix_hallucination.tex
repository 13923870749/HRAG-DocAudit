\section{Lenient vs.\ strict hallucination rates}
\label{app:hallucination}

Lenient hallucination counts an unsupported positive (\texttt{pred=1}, \texttt{label=0}) only when the method lacks valid retrieval evidence; rule-routed cascade decisions are treated as auditable rule outputs. Strict hallucination counts every false positive regardless of routing path.

Strict counting exposes rule-engine false positives that lenient counting excludes (e.g., 7.5\% on C3PA rule-amenable items for \HRAG{} and OR-ensemble). Both metrics are therefore reported together when comparing safety claims. In particular, \HRAG{} and OR-ensemble are identical under the strict metric on every reported stratum; any claim of reduced hallucination is restricted to RAG-origin (lenient) unsupported positives on rule-routed items.
